From Student Data to an Action Plan

A systematic 4-step pipeline designed to translate student credentials into actionable career roadmaps.

01

Build Your Profile

Students provide inputs across CGPA, branch, internships, projects, certifications, coding scores, aptitude, and attendance.

02

Predict Readiness

The calibrated machine-learning model analyzes the multidimensional profile and generates a placement probability.

03

Identify Risk Areas

The system flags potential vulnerabilities and assigns an intervention priority (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).

04

Get AI Guidance

The AI Career Advisor prescribes tailored roadmaps for coding drills, resume tailoring, and behavioral interview prep.
